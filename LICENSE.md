Changes: the material was converted into pages with fields and sections. Names are written in natural order, with the name the SRD gives as an alias when it differs (Leather Armor for "Leather", Hempen Rope for "Rope, hempen (50 feet)"), and as the stat blocks write them. Units are written without the final period (40 ft, 3 lb). Potion of Healing joins the adventuring gear entry and the magic item. The section "In the campaign" of Weapon, +1, +2, or +3 is ours, not the SRD's.

The rules in `rules/` are the text of the SRD 5.1 in markdown, with its headings, tables, bold and italics, and its words unchanged. Adventuring and Combat are titles of ours: in the SRD their sections follow those of Using Ability Scores without a title of their own. Some chapters are taken in part: Equipment up to its weapons (pages 62–65), Spellcasting without its spell lists and spells (pages 100–104). In the Travel Pace table, the heading "Distance Traveled per..." is written out in each of its three columns.

rpg-engine is compatible with fifth edition.
