# The 5e system: license

The pages in `world/`, the rules in `rules/` and the game's data in `system.yaml` (its coins, conditions, the length of a round, the titles of its rules, its proficiency table, the names of its skills, how its numbers are worked out, and the uses of features with the rests that bring them back) are material from the System Reference Document 5.1, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC-BY-4.0). The rest of rpg-engine is under the MIT license.

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 ("SRD 5.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. Changes: the material was converted into pages with fields and sections. Names are written in natural order, with the name the SRD gives as an alias when it differs (Leather Armor for "Leather", Hempen Rope for "Rope, hempen (50 feet)", Sledgehammer for "Hammer, sledge", Stone of Good Luck for "Stone of Good Luck (Luckstone)"), a saddle with its kind (Exotic Saddle), and as the stat blocks write them. Units are written without the final period (40 ft, 3 lb). Potion of Healing joins the adventuring gear entry and the magic item, with the gear entry's sentence on drinking it after the magic item's text. A stat block within an item is set off as a quotation.

Ability modifiers, the proficiency bonus, saving throws, skills, initiative, passive Perception, Insight and Investigation, the hit point maximum and the hit dice are written as formulas in `system.yaml`, with what Jack of All Trades, Remarkable Athlete and Aura of Protection add to them; the columns of a class's table become fields of the sheet at the character's level; a spellcasting class's page gives its kind of caster and its save DC, attack and prepared spells as formulas, and the Multiclass Spellcaster table gives the spell slots by caster level; a creature's saving throws and skills read from the totals its stat block lists, and its challenge and experience points are two fields, cr and xp-value; skill names become field names (Sleight of Hand becomes sleight-of-hand). Races and subraces are pages that give their traits as fields where they are numbers or lists, and keep their text; a subrace's Ability Score Increase is titled with its name, such as Ability Score Increase (Hill Dwarf), so that it stands beside its race's. A class page opens with its table, without the label over the spell slot columns; a subclass page names its class and lists its features with the level at which each comes, as its text gives them; the uses of Breath Weapon and Relentless Endurance are counters on their race's page; the Warlock's invocations stand under the feature that names them; the background is Acolyte (background), beside the creature Acolyte, its own parts as sections and the chapter's general text after it; the Feats chapter's text opens the page of Grappler. The spells were converted from the text of the SRD by a script: their fields come from the lines of their header, the spell lists (pages 105–113) become spell-lists, and their text is unchanged, without its italics, with its run-in titles in bold italics and its tables; Antipathy/Sympathy, Blindness/Deafness and Enlarge/Reduce are named with a hyphen for the slash, which a file name cannot hold, and Shield is Shield (spell), beside the item. Each class's spell list (pages 105–113) is a page of its own, such as Wizard Spells, with a section for each level and a link to each spell. The armor, the shield and the weapons of the Armor and Weapons tables (pages 63–66) are pages written by a script from those tables, named in natural order with the SRD's name as an alias (Light Crossbow for "Crossbow, light"): an armor's Armor Class, a shield's bonus, and a weapon's attack and damage are formulas on who wears or wields them, with a magic bonus of 0 that a magic item of the campaign writes; the Strength and Stealth columns are fields; the text of each armor and of the Lance and the Net is the SRD's. The rest of the Equipment chapter (pages 66–74) and the Magic Items chapter (pages 206–253) were converted by scripts from the lines of the PDF, with their fonts and positions. Each piece of adventuring gear, equipment pack, tool, tack and vehicle is a page with the columns of its table as fields (cost, weight, speed), the length its name gives (50 feet for a rope) and the uses of the healer's kit, and as text the description under its run-in title, or its group's (a crystal takes the Arcane Focus's, the keelboat and the rowboat Rowed Vessels'); the row that heads a group in a table gives its rows their category, such as arcane focus, but the disguise kit and the forgery kit, which the PDF indents under Artisan's tools, are tools of their own; the mounts, the trade goods, the expenses and the stabling are in the rules alone. Each magic item is a page with the category, rarity and attunement of its type line as fields, and its text unchanged, with its italics, run-in titles, lists, tables and the stat blocks set in it, such as the Avatar of Death; in the table of the Iron Flask, the PDF's figure dashes are en dashes, as in the other tables.

The rules in `rules/` are the text of the SRD 5.1 in markdown, with its headings, tables, bold and italics, and its words unchanged: a heading is one level below the nearest larger one above it in the PDF, and the title of a sidebar is at the level of the headings beside it. Adventuring and Combat are titles of ours: in the SRD their sections follow those of Using Ability Scores without a title of their own. Traps, Diseases, Madness, Objects and Poisons close Spellcasting in the SRD, after its spells, without a chapter of their own (pages 195–205): each is a chapter here, its headings one level up. Some chapters are taken in part: Spellcasting without its spell lists and spells (pages 100–104), and Magic Items without its items, which are pages, and so without the heading Artifacts, which holds only the Orb of Dragonkind. The sidebars Equipment Packs and Self-Sufficiency are sections, and a row that heads a group in a table, in italics in the PDF, is in bold, as in the Armor and Weapons tables. Beyond 1st Level is taken up to its backgrounds (pages 56–60), which the page Acolyte (background) holds; Races, Monsters and Appendix MM-B: Nonplayer Characters without their races and stat blocks, which are pages (pages 3, 254–260, 395), Monsters keeping as a section of its own the Half-Dragon Template that stands among its stat blocks (pages 320–321). In the Travel Pace table, the heading "Distance Traveled per..." is written out in each of its three columns. The tables Proficiency Bonus by Challenge Rating and Experience Points by Challenge Rating, printed in two halves side by side (pages 256, 258), are each one table of two columns, and in Appendix PH-B each table of deities follows its pantheon's text (pages 360–362). The run-in title X/Day (page 259) is not in bold italics, since a title cannot hold a slash. A sidebar that falls among the parts of a section closes it, at the section's level, as Armor, Weapon, and Tool Proficiencies after Senses (page 257). Appendix MM-A's creatures are pages; its two lines of introduction (page 366) are left out.
