# The 5e system: license

The pages in `world/`, the rules in `rules/` and the game's data in `system.yaml` (its coins, conditions, the length of a round, the titles of its rules, its proficiency table, the names of its skills, and how its numbers are worked out) are material from the System Reference Document 5.1, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC-BY-4.0). The SRD 5.1 notes: "Section 5 of CC-BY-4.0 includes a Disclaimer of Warranties and Limitation of Liability that limits our liability to you."

Changes: the material was converted into pages with fields and sections. Names are written in natural order, with the name the SRD gives as an alias when it differs (Leather Armor for "Leather", Hempen Rope for "Rope, hempen (50 feet)"), and as the stat blocks write them. Units are written without the final period (40 ft, 3 lb). Potion of Healing joins the adventuring gear entry and the magic item. The section "In the campaign" of Weapon, +1, +2, or +3 is ours, not the SRD's.

Ability modifiers, the proficiency bonus, saving throws, skills, initiative, passive Perception, the hit point maximum and the hit dice are written as formulas in `system.yaml`, a creature's saving throws and skills read from the totals its stat block lists; skill names become field names (Sleight of Hand becomes sleight-of-hand). Races and subraces are pages that give their traits as fields where they are numbers or lists, and keep their text; a subrace's Ability Score Increase is titled with its name, such as Ability Score Increase (Hill Dwarf), so that it stands beside its race's.

The rules in `rules/` are the text of the SRD 5.1 in markdown, with its headings, tables, bold and italics, and its words unchanged. Adventuring and Combat are titles of ours: in the SRD their sections follow those of Using Ability Scores without a title of their own. Some chapters are taken in part: Equipment up to its weapons (pages 62–65), Spellcasting without its spell lists and spells (pages 100–104). In the Travel Pace table, the heading "Distance Traveled per..." is written out in each of its three columns.

rpg-engine is compatible with fifth edition.
