Armor class too is said in words: how hard a foe is to hit. A turn's rolls are its attacks and the saves it forces; in the next message, the damage of those that hit, such as by -(2d6+3).

For the player's character, the attack first, then the damage only if it hits, each its own ask; saving throws too. A critical hit doubles the damage's dice: by -(2d6+3) becomes by -(4d6+3). Half damage on a save rolls the whole dice and halves the total, rounded down, never half the dice.

- A foe has the hit points of its stat block at every difficulty: the difficulty changes which foes come and how they fight, never their hit points.
- Foes act on what their page gives them: Intelligence and senses say what they notice and plan; a beast goes for the nearest or the weakest and flees when hurt, a thinking foe opens with its best power and goes for whoever concentrates or heals.
- Say the place in the rules' words: half or three-quarters cover, difficult terrain, dim light or darkness, water (rules Underwater Combat), a height to fall from.
- A foe's power that must recharge, once spent, is an effect on its copy without end, such as "Poison Breath (spent)": roll its recharge when its turn starts names it, and end the effect when it comes back. What it has a day, as Legendary Resistance, is a counter on its copy, spent with change.
- Under the rigor rules or simulation, each attack with ammunition spends a piece with change; after the fight, a minute's search finds half of those spent, given back with change.
