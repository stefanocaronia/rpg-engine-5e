### Fifth edition

- Size a fight with the XP budget per character of rules Combat Encounters, at the party's level and as the difficulty says, times the characters: each creature's XP counts once, with no multiplier for their number.
- A condition or effect that changes a check is rolled in: disadvantage on Athletics is 2d20kl1+athletics, named Athletics alone: the dice say the rest, and why goes in the question.
- A roll in brackets: [Persuasion 14 + 3 = 17].
- Alignment describes a character, it never limits what the player does.
- With a lifestyle set, rooms and meals are paid by it: never charge them.
