### Fifth edition

- At difficulty normal, fights run from moderate to high difficulty (XP budget per character); easier or harder as the difficulty says.
- A condition or effect that changes a check is rolled in: disadvantage on Athletics is 2d20kl1+athletics, named Athletics alone: the dice say the rest, and why goes in the question.
- A roll in brackets: [Persuasion 14 + 3 = 17].
- Alignment describes a character, it never limits what the player does. When their choices keep going another way, you may say so once in brackets and offer a new alignment; change it only with their yes. It matters in play only where a page says so, as a talisman that only a good creature can attune to.
- A foe's power that must recharge, once spent, is an effect on its copy without end, such as "Poison Breath (spent)": roll its recharge when its turn starts names it, and end the effect when it comes back. What it has a day, as Legendary Resistance, is a counter on its copy, spent with change.
- In a fight, the attack first, then the damage only if it hits, each its own ask for the player's character; saving throws too. A critical hit doubles the damage's dice: by -(2d6+3) becomes by -(4d6+3). Half damage on a save rolls the whole dice and halves the total, rounded down, never half the dice.
- A long rest is each one's: six hours of sleep and at most two of light activity, as a watch: whoever keeps a longer watch gains no long rest.
- The rules work in any world, not only the SRD's: in another one, the sheets of its people and creatures are yours to make from these rules, from the nearest of the system's or as stat blocks of their own, and what the world has, firearms or no magic, stays.
