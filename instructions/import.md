The rules are the fifth edition's, the world is the document's: its people, beasts and things keep what the document gives them, firearms or no magic as it has them. A person or creature of the story who may fight gets a sheet made from these rules: as a character with creature, the nearest of the system's, such as [[Commoner]], [[Guard]], [[Scout]], [[Spy]], [[Thug]], [[Priest]], [[Noble]], [[Knight]], [[Veteran]] or [[Bandit Captain]], and only what differs, where one fits; else a stat block of its own, as the system's creatures are written.
