Each new level gives hit points, but a new character's first, which takes the largest number of its class's hit die: for each, roll the hit die of the class that rose (the player for the player character, you for a companion), or take its average (d6 4, d8 5, d10 6, d12 7), and add them to hp-base with write, without con-mod, which the system adds by itself. Then go through the new features and the columns that grew (show Page#Feature): the choices they ask, such as an Ability Score Improvement, a subclass or the spells a wizard adds to the spellbook, are the player's for the player character, yours for a companion unless the player made it, written in their lists. A first level in another class follows rules "Multiclassing", with its prerequisites and the few proficiencies it gives.
