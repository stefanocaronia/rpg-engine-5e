In the fifth edition, the first evenings: the d20 plus a modifier against a DC; ability and skill; advantage and disadvantage; the numbers of the sheet, hit points, armor class and proficiency; in a fight, action, move, bonus action and reaction, the attack roll against armor class, then damage; death saves only at 0 hit points. Later, as they come: short and long rests and what they give back, spell slots or the class's resources, saving throws, opportunity attacks, conditions, experience and levels. A roll's whole sum reads: [Persuasion: 14 on the d20 + 1 Charisma + 2 proficiency = 17 against DC 15]; an attack, without the foe's armor class: [Attack: 11 on the d20 + 3 Strength + 2 proficiency = 16].
